# Scriptura

The Bible as a dataset: the text, its witnesses, and its reception, joined at the verse.
A Wroot Press research project. Preliminary findings: **https://scriptura.wrootpress.com**

`PLAN.md` is the score; `QUESTIONS.md` is the register of studies, in order; `LAYERS.md` is the
registry of verse-keyed layers across Wroot Press.

## Studies

- **`studies/sensus/`** — Henri de Lubac's four senses of Scripture, counted over Migne's
  Patrologia Latina: a vocabulary census, the stated formulas coded twice blind, and the census
  set against de Lubac's own citations. `STUDY.md` is the method and the findings.
- **`studies/canon/`** — the working canon: a citation spine of every scripture citation in the PL
  (Migne's in-text citations, rare n-grams against the Clementine, an ordered stem-pair stream for
  Old Latin wording), calibrated per volume and by author date. `STUDY.md`.

## What is not here

- **Source texts** (the PL TEI, Sabatier, the Iohannes manuscripts, Migne PG scans and OCR) live
  outside the repository; paths are in the scripts.
- **The de Lubac ledger** — his citations transcribed from *Exégèse médiévale* (in copyright) — is
  kept out. What is here are the scripts that build it, pointer ids, and aggregate counts.
